On Sat, Aug 22, 2026 at 11:48:59AM +0300, Dmitry Baryshkov wrote:
> On targets (like A530 / MSM8996) without per-process pgtables
> msm_gpu_create_private_vm() uses the global VM, so all DRM files share
> one GPU address space.

A306 (MSM8916) is hit by this too, from the compositor side: phoc
(wlroots) samples a client's window buffer through a dma-buf, and when
the client frees that buffer -- on its first resize at start-up or when
it exits -- phoc's next composite reads the torn-down iova. On a Samsung
Galaxy Tab A 9.7 (SM-T550) that showed up as bursts of thousands of

  *** fault: iova=..., flags=0

on the GPU context (1f09000.iommu-ctx), roughly once every 20-60 GTK4
application launches. An ftrace of iommu:unmap and iommu:io_page_fault
put the faulting range inside a 2.5 MB window buffer whose mapping the
exiting client had just removed.

With both patches of this series on top of 7.3-rc2 (msm8916-mainline
tree), 180 launch/close cycles of six GTK4 apps gave no GPU faults;
without them the same loop faulted after 20 and 57 launches. Given the
reference-loop concern, I also kprobed msm_gem_free_object() over two
camera-app sessions: every BO whose last handle and dma-buf were gone
was freed (the objects left over all still had an open handle).

The testing was done with the help of an AI assistant.
