On Sat, Sep 26, 2026 at 04:17:13PM +1000, Sam Day wrote:
> A306 and A306A implement only three VBIF XIN halt request bits.
> Consequently, waiting for the full 6-bit A3xx mask times out and
> prevents runtime suspend.

Same on a Samsung Galaxy Tab A 9.7 (SM-T550, MSM8916, A306 chip id
0x03000600): writing 0x3f to VBIF_XIN_HALT_CTRL0 reads back 0x7, and
VBIF_XIN_HALT_CTRL1 reads 0x00070007 right away. Without the patch the
GPU never runtime-suspends (runtime_suspended_time stays at 0) and
a3xx_pm_suspend() is retried from a kworker every 66 ms, which kept
CPU usage noticeably up on this board.

With this patch applied to 7.3-rc2 (the msm8916-mainline tree) the GPU
suspends whenever it is idle. About 370 runtime suspend/resume cycles
under GTK4 GL apps, camera preview, glmark2 and DPMS off/on showed no
GPU hangs, and no more GPU faults than with runtime PM disabled.

The testing was done with the help of an AI assistant.
