Hi David,

I ran into the same seek stall on MSM8916 (HFI 1.x, firmware
VIDEO.VE.1.8) and ended up with an equivalent change, so thanks for
posting this. On that firmware it needs one more piece, though.

Between the CAPTURE STREAMON and the OUTPUT STREAMON, the capture
buffers userspace queues go straight to the firmware from
venus_helper_vb2_buf_queue() (start_streaming_called is already set),
but they also stay in the m2m context. The new SEEK path then submits
them a second time through venus_helper_process_initial_cap_bufs().
GStreamer queues buffers in that window on almost every seek, and the
1.x firmware answers with "Err_Fatal - ...\venus\utils\src\vbuffer.c:623"
followed by a system error and recovery (7 of them in one test session,
none without the patch).

Keeping capture buffers in the m2m context while the decoder is in SEEK
and OUTPUT is off fixes that here; the diff on top of your patch is
below. With both, flushing seeks to 0 s and 10 s in GStreamer playbin3,
and seeking in Firefox (FFmpeg h264_v4l2m2m), play on without firmware
errors. Seeks also exposed a second, independent bug (stale parked
capture buffers after STREAMOFF), for which I'm sending "media: venus:
vdec: drop parked capture buffers on capture STREAMOFF" separately.

If you fold this into v2, feel free to add:

Tested-by: Yaron Shahrabani <EMAIL> # SM-T550 (MSM8916, HFI 1.x)

This was found and tested with the help of an AI assistant; I reviewed
and tested the change myself.

Thanks,
Yaron

+	 * During a seek, keep capture buffers in the m2m context until the
